# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences with equal weight:
- Recruiters and companies evaluating Mateo as a junior web developer: they skim projects, download the CV and reach out.
- Freelance clients (small businesses and entrepreneurs: gyms, jewellery shops, people with several ventures) who want a website, management system or WhatsApp bot built for them.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Jorge Mateo Gimenez, Técnico en Programación. It shows who he is, what he works with and real projects (some built for paying clients), and turns the visit into contact: CV download, LinkedIn/GitHub, or the contact form.

## Positioning

A junior developer who has already shipped work for real clients (jewellery stock management, gym site, WhatsApp bot for a multi-venture owner), not only practice projects.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, the CV or a direct link, on desktop and phone. Deployed on Vercel (https://portafolio-silk-two-32.vercel.app/). Spanish-language site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Astro 5 + Tailwind 3, static site.
- Contact form posts to formsubmit.co (matex23gimenez11@gmail.com).
- Project data lives in `src/data/proyectos.json`, skills in `src/data/skills.json`.
- Copy may be rewritten for clarity, but no invented facts, metrics, clients or testimonials.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Gimenez Jorge Mateo. Title: Técnico en Programación / desarrollador web.
- Stack named in the bio: JavaScript, React, Node.js.
- Tone: friendly, first person, Spanish (rioplatense-neutral).

## Product Principles

1. Projects are the proof: real work leads, claims follow.
2. Every section ends one step away from contact.
3. Personality is welcome; it must never hide the work or slow the page.
4. Works as well on a phone as on a desktop.
